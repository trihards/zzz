A taste match. A little judgment.

Your answers combine the comedy you enjoy with the people and group dynamics that keep you watching. Matches use both comedy profiles and contestant roles, then reduce confrontational picks if you prefer gentler humor.

This edition covers all 21 completed UK series, from series 1 through 21, with 32 curated gateway videos across 14 series. Series 22 is still airing and is not profiled yet. The profiles are editorial interpretations of each cast’s comedy mix. This is not a scientific personality test. No winner spoilers are included in our descriptions.

Contestant roles describe how someone comes across within that particular cast. People can have more than one role, and those roles develop as the group gets to know each other. They are flexible editorial interpretations. The cast introductions describe broad comic styles without revealing results or specific moments.

Series and clip matches are scored separately: one memorable task cannot represent an entire cast. Clip roles describe the encounter in that video; series matches consider the whole group. The cast profiles draw on reviews and fan discussion; the judgments and quiz scoring are ours.

“More like this” and “Try something different” adjust both recommendation modes for this visit. Quiz answers, exclusions, and reactions are held in page memory only and disappear when you reload. We do not save preferences or use site analytics.

Runtimes are for the linked uploads. “Quick hit” means four minutes or less; the other labels distinguish full tasks, highlights, studio tasks, and extended versions.

Videos open from official Taskmaster or broadcaster uploads. Some are full or extended tasks. Playback and availability vary by country.
